BUILDER'S NOTES

Make your
first one easier.

1

Use sharp scissors. Small, clean cuts make a surprisingly big difference.

2

Fold the lines before adding glue. It makes assembly much easier.

3

Use a small amount of glue. Too much can soften the paper.

4

Let the glue set before moving your finished papercraft.
