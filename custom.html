THE STORY OF 1RS.SHOP

PAPER IS
ENOUGH.

We believe you don't need expensive materials to make something worth keeping. One sheet of paper can become a house, a character, a vehicle, a gift or an entire little world.

MADE FROM ONE SHEET

Start
small.

1rs.shop begins with an intentionally unusual idea: make papercraft accessible to almost everyone. The ₹1 entry point isn't about making paper less valuable. It's about removing the hesitation to try something new.

Build
slowly.

A digital screen gives you an image in seconds. Papercraft asks you to slow down. Cut a line. Fold a tab. Add a little glue. Then suddenly something exists because you made it.

Keep
it.

Our goal isn't just to sell a printed sheet. We want the finished object to sit on a desk, shelf or bedside table and remind you that you made it yourself.

Make it
yours.

Beyond our collection, we're building custom papercrafts where your photos, characters, memories and ideas can become physical paper objects.

OUR MISSION

TURN ORDINARY
PAPER INTO
EXTRAORDINARY THINGS.

1rs.shop exists to make creativity physical. We design papercrafts that are simple enough to build, interesting enough to display and affordable enough to experiment with. The sheet arrives flat. The imagination does the rest.
