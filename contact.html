Quick answers.

Is the papercraft really ₹1?

The selected papercraft starts at ₹1. Delivery and any optional upsells are calculated separately at checkout.

What do I receive?

The physical product is printed on A5 paper and prepared for you to cut, fold and build.

Do you make custom papercrafts?

Yes. Visit the Custom Papercraft page to start with your own photo or idea.

How does delivery work?

Your printed sheet is packed and shipped to the address provided during checkout. Shipping charges are shown separately.

Can I order multiple designs?

Yes. You can add multiple papercrafts to your basket before checkout.

I received a damaged package. What now?

Contact us with your order details and photos of the package so we can help resolve the issue.
